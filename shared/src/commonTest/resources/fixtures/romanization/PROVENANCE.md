# Independent public-domain song corpus

Complete short traditional song texts, independent from the first-party inflection lexicon. Accessed 2026-10-23.

1. Sakura Sakura, unknown author, 1888 edition of Sōkyokushū, transcription via https://ja.wikisource.org/w/index.php?title=さくらさくら&oldid=132823 . Source marks the work public domain worldwide. Fixture removes parenthesized furigana annotations, splits phrases into lines, expands historical repeat mark in いざや〳〵 to いざやいざや. Historical orthography is preserved. Backend output is dictionary pronunciation, not a claim to reproduce historical sung pronunciation.
2. Mo Li Hua, traditional Jiangsu song, version recorded in Zhui Bai Qiu (1763–1774), via https://zh.wikisource.org/w/index.php?title=茉莉花&oldid=331764 . Source marks it public domain worldwide. Phrase line breaks added; characters/punctuation preserved. Historical text, not modern authored adaptation.

Golden assertions check complete conversion, no Han/kana residue, unresolved ranges empty, text/timing/occurrence preservation and independent phrase readings. Unknown-script cases are separate; no failing corpus line is silently removed.
